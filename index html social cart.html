How it works

You bring the snacks. We bring the cart.

The Social Cart is a dry rental — the styled wooden cart itself, with tin serving trays built in. Delivery, setup, and pickup are on us; the food, drinks, and serving are on you.

  • IncludedThe cart, delivery, setup & pickup, letter board for a custom sign
  • Not includedFood, drinks, ice, serving staff, and decor beyond the cart

Want help planning what goes on it? Mention that in your request and we'll point you in the right direction.

Good to know before you book

  • This is a cart rental, not a catering service — food, drinks, ice, and serving are up to you.
  • No appliances, open flames, propane, or hot/heated items are allowed on the cart. It's built for cold and room-temperature snacks only.
  • Please let us know your venue's setup restrictions (indoor/outdoor, power access, stairs) when you request your quote.
  • A confirmed booking requires your event date, location, and headcount.
